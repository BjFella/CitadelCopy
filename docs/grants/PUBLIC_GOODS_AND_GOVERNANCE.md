# Public-goods and governance commitment

Citadel is MIT licensed. The operation contracts, adapters, benchmark methods,
raw privacy-safe cells, negative results, signatures, reports, documentation,
and reproduction commands funded by the grant remain public.

## Publication rules

- Freeze tasks, policies, metrics, gates, and verifiers before measured work.
- Publish passed, failed, unknown, interrupted, and adversarial cells.
- Preserve a failed benchmark under its original identity.
- Use a new identity for any post-result method change.
- Keep private keys and raw sensitive provider output outside the repository.
- Publish public keys, digests, source bindings, and bounded observations.
- Treat provider invoice, subscription allocation, market-equivalent price,
  local energy, amortization, and actual cash as different lenses.

## Maintenance rules

- Version public contracts and retain compatibility fixtures.
- Require adapter conformance before calling a runtime supported.
- Keep beginner installation and `/do` behavior independent of research mode.
- Generate public claims from the canonical evidence manifest.
- Run offline proof reconstruction on pull requests and hosted infrastructure.
- Document security, privacy, contribution, and release boundaries.

## Governance direction

Seth remains the initial maintainer and release authority. The funded work will
make authority inspectable rather than implicit: signed releases, reviewed
contract changes, migration notes, reproducible artifacts, and public failure
reports. No token, proprietary service, or single hosted endpoint is required
to inspect or reproduce the public evidence.
