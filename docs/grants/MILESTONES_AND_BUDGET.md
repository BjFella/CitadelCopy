## Release gates

1. **Integrity:** zero false passes, signature failures, chain failures, or
   identity substitutions.
2. **Quality:** adaptive reaches at least 95% of the strongest baseline's
   independently verified completion.
3. **Economics:** savings use measured end-to-end components or remain blocked.
4. **Portability:** three open-stack adapters pass identical conformance.
5. **Reproducibility:** privacy-safe raw cells, code, freeze, reports, and
   offline verification are public.

Failure is an allowed milestone output. Changing a task, verifier, metric, or
threshold after results requires a new benchmark identity and preserves the
prior result.
