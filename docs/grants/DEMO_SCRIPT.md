# Two-minute Citadel application walkthrough

The demo should be screen-first, captioned, and usable without sound. Do not
compress the research story into fast terminal noise.

## 0:00-0:25 — First value

- Open the homepage hero: “Start with `/do`. Scale when the work demands it.”
- Enter `/do review README.md`.
- Show the proportional route and independent verification expectation.
- Caption: **Citadel works around the coding agent you already use.**

## 0:25-0:50 — Work survives the chat

- Advance the campaign scenario.
- Close and restore through `/do next`.
- Show the repository state and next action.
- Caption: **The durable unit is the repository contract, not the chat.**

## 0:50-1:20 — Control and proof

- Open Operation Control.
- Show requested model/topology, observed identity, required artifacts, and the
  external verifier.
- Switch to the failed attempts and show that they remain failed.
- Caption: **A runtime cannot promote its own status into proof.**

## 1:20-1:50 — The economic result

- Open Research and the 72-cell prospective comparison.
- Show adaptive 27/36 versus baseline 24/36 and 9.9% less measured GPU energy.
- Show the failed 30% economic gate.
- Caption: **Citadel improved this result and still rejected its own savings claim.**

## 1:50-2:00 — Funding target

- Show the funded gate: at least 95% frontier verified completion and at least
  30% lower measured end-to-end cost across multiple stacks.
- End on the one-command evidence verification link.

## Capture requirements

- 1920×1080 master, 30 fps, H.264 MP4.
- 16:9 captions inside safe margins; no burned-in tiny terminal text.
- No synthetic user testimonial, fake live activity, or unverified savings.
- Provide an animated GIF or three-image fallback for application fields that
  do not accept video.
